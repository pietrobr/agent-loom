## Biografia

Hi, I’m a cloud security consultant at Microsoft, where I help organizations protect their data and systems. I’m passionate about identifying security vulnerabilities and working to remediate them effectively.Before joining Microsoft, I worked as a web application penetration tester, assessing the security of web apps using tools such as Burp Suite and others. I also have a background in software development and built simple applications using C#.I hold a degree in Computer Science.Outside of work, I love traveling and discovering new places, I’m an animal lover, and a video game enthusiast. I have three dogs and more than one gaming console that I enjoy playing on during the weekends. I’m always happy to chat about technology, cybersecurity, travel, or gaming.
