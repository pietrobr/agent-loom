## Biografia

Silvio Di Benedetto is the Founder and CEO of Inside Technologies, an Italian company specializing in cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and digital transformation. With over 20 years of experience in the IT industry, he helps organizations modernize operations, improve resilience, and ensure 24/7 business continuity.He has collaborated with leading vendors such as Microsoft, Veeam, Parallels, and Netwrix, producing technical content, training sessions, and videos for IT professionals worldwide. Recognized as a Microsoft Most Valuable Professional (MVP) in Cloud and Datacenter Management since 2012 and a Very Important Parallels Person (VIPP) since 2016, Silvio also leads WindowServer.it and co-founded AperiTeams, two major communities dedicated to IT professionals and innovation.
