## Biografia

I am a seasoned IT professional with over two decades of experience specializing in Unified Communication and Modern Workplace solutions. Throughout my career, I have been recognized for my technical expertise and community contributions across Microsoft Teams, Office 365, Skype for Business, Lync, and Active Directory.I was honoured with multiple Microsoft MVP awards between 2011 and 2017, reflecting my deep engagement with the Microsoft ecosystem and my commitment to knowledge sharing.In addition to authoring technical books and articles, I am an experienced speaker and regular presenter at industry events. I also serve as one of the organizers of BeConnected Day, one of the leading community conferences in Italy focused on Microsoft 365 and modern collaboration. This role has allowed me to help shape content for a wide audience of IT professionals and to foster connections within the Microsoft community.
