## Biografia

At the age of 5, thanks to a Philips PC and MS-DOS, I was introduced to the world of computer science. And here I am today, armed with a pair of nerd glasses and various devices, in this "cloud" world.I work at Microsoft as an Endpoint Cloud Solution Architect. I have over 15 years of experience with Microsoft technologies and, in my current role, I support clients in deriving the maximum value from Microsoft Intune, Winows 365 and Azure Virtual Desktop, removing any obstacles that might hinder optimal use.I publish Microsoft-related technical content on my newsletter ITSpecialist.news and, together with other enthusiasts, I manage the "Microsoft Security Italian Users Group" community on LinkedIn. This community is dedicated to Microsoft technologies in the field of Identity & Security, where we organize events, share industry updates, and publish technical articles.
