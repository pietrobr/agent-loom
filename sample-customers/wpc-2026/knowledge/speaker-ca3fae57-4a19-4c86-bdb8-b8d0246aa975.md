Francesco Valerio Buccoli is a Cybersecurity expert, working within the Security Service Line of the Industry Solutions division at Microsoft. He specializes in designing cloud solutions for enterprise customers, developing comprehensive security architectures and strategies to safeguard critical assets and data. With over a decade of experience as a speaker and a Microsoft Certified Trainer, Francesco shares his expertise with audiences, empowering them with valuable insights and practical knowledge in the ever-evolving fields of Cloud Computing and Cybersecurity.
