## Biografia

Angelo Gulisano is the Founder and Lead IT Consultant at Angelo Gulisano S.r.l., Microsoft MVP in Business Applications, Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT), and international speaker. He specializes in Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Power Platform, and AI-driven business solutions, helping organizations turn emerging technologies into practical, production-ready applications. With extensive hands-on experience delivering enterprise projects, Angelo focuses on sharing real-world architectures, lessons learned, and best practices that attendees can immediately apply in their own organizations.
