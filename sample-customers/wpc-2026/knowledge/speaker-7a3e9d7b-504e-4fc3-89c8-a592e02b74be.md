## Biografia

Andrea Uggetti is a Cloud Solution Architect – Data & AI at Microsoft, specializing in Microsoft Fabric, Azure Databricks, and AI-powered analytics solutions.He works with large international enterprises to design and implement modern data platforms that combine analytics, governance, real-time intelligence, and artificial intelligence. His expertise spans Microsoft Fabric, Azure Databricks, Power BI, Lakehouse/warehouse architectures, and enterprise-scale data modernization.Andrea collaborates closely with Microsoft engineering teams, including both the Analysis Services and Microsoft Fabric product groups, contributing customer insights, architectural guidance, technical reviews, and product feedback that help shape the evolution of Microsoft's data platform.With a background in Business Intelligence dating back to the early releases of SQL Server Analysis Services, he brings a unique perspective that connects traditional analytics with the emerging world of Generative AI and Agentic AI.Today, his primary focus is helping organizations build intelligent data platforms that leverage AI Skills, Data Agents, Copilot experiences, and conversational analytics to transform how users interact with enterprise data. He is passionate about enabling the next generation of data-driven applications through Microsoft Fabric, Azure Databricks, and the Microsoft AI ecosystem.
