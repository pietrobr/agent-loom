# Aurelia Motors — Demo Drive Playbook

Fictional demo material for Client Advisors.

## VENTO-PASS: Vento demonstration drive

Before confirming a Vento demonstration drive, the Client Advisor checks the
demo-car calendar with the Atelier coordinator. Record the model, preferred
time, and whether the client wants a short introduction to the retractable
hardtop. A tentative slot is not a confirmed booking.

The Atelier coordinator owns **VENTO-PASS**, the demo-drive readiness check.
Before handing over the keys, the coordinator confirms that the demo car is
available, the route briefing is complete, and an advisor can accompany the
drive. If any check is missing, keep the slot tentative and offer a showroom
walkthrough instead.

After the drive, record one feature the client liked and one open question.
Agree on a follow-up date before closing the appointment.
