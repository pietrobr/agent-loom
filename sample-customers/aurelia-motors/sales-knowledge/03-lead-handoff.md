# Aurelia Motors — Lead Handoff

Fictional demo material for Client Advisors. Use placeholder lead IDs in demos.

## MODENA-48: handoff to the Atelier

Use **MODENA-48** when a lead wants a bespoke consultation or factory
collection walkthrough that the original Client Advisor cannot schedule
directly. The receiving role is the **Atelier Guest Relations Coordinator**.
The original Client Advisor remains the point of contact until the coordinator
accepts the handoff.

Include the demo lead ID, model of interest, requested experience, preferred
appointment window, and the next step already agreed. Do not include an
identity document, payment details, or contact details in a demo record.

The coordinator acknowledges the handoff within **two business days**. If
there is no acknowledgment, the Client Advisor follows up with the
coordinator. Do not tell the lead that an appointment is booked until the
Atelier confirms a slot.
