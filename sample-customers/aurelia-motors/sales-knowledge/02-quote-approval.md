# Aurelia Motors — Quote Approval

Fictional demo material for Client Advisors. This document contains no real
pricing, discounts, or commercial terms.

## ORCHID-27: quote release approval

Use **ORCHID-27** when a draft quote includes a custom paint or other
non-catalogue bespoke item. The **Atelier Sales Manager** signs off on the
quote before it is sent to the client. A Client Advisor can prepare the draft
but cannot release it before the manager approves it.

Mark the draft **Pending Atelier Review**. Include the model, the requested
bespoke specification, and any unconfirmed lead-time assumptions. Ask the
Bespoke team to confirm feasibility and flag anything that is still open.
After sign-off, the Client Advisor sends the approved version. If the
specification changes, return the draft to Pending Atelier Review.
